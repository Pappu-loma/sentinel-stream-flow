# Sentinel AI

Project: Sentinel — a premium financial AI forecasting product

Design a two-part web experience: a cinematic landing page, and a chat-style application interface. Visual theme throughout: dark chrome / liquid mercury. Think a black mirror with a metallic sheen — deep black and charcoal backgrounds, with silver, white, and gunmetal-gray surfaces that catch light like polished liquid metal. Strictly monochrome — no color accents anywhere (no blue, no gold, no green/red status colors either). All visual interest comes from light, reflection, gradient, and depth — not color.

Mascot: Silver Surfer — a liquid chrome/silver humanoid figure — is the visual identity of the product. He should appear prominently throughout, rendered in the same polished liquid-metal material language as the rest of the UI, so he feels like he's made of the same "substance" as the interface itself.

PART 1 — Landing Page

A premium, cinematic, scroll-driven marketing page, in the style of high-end product launch sites (Apple product pages, premium SaaS landers). Content should progressively reveal as the user scrolls — not all visible at once.

Hero section: Full-bleed, dramatic. Silver Surfer as the central visual — large, liquid-metal rendered, dominating the frame with dramatic studio-style lighting (rim light, soft reflections, subtle specular highlights). Minimal text — just a product name/wordmark and a short tagline. Deep black background with the figure catching light like polished chrome.

Scroll behavior: As the user scrolls, Silver Surfer should move, shift pose, or transform in sync with scroll position — a parallax or cross-fade sequence through different poses/angles, giving the sense of him animating through the page as you scroll (similar to scroll-linked 3D/character sequences on premium product sites).

Story sections (as many as feels right for a polished narrative arc): what the product does, the problem it solves, how it works at a high level, and a closing call-to-action inviting the user into the app. Each section should feel spacious, with generous negative space, large confident typography, and metallic UI accents (thin glowing borders, subtle chrome gradients on buttons/cards) rather than flat colored blocks.

Typography: Clean, modern, high-contrast sans-serif. Confident and minimal — think premium tech branding, not corporate/dashboard-y.

Micro-interactions: Buttons and interactive elements should have a liquid, fluid hover response — like mercury reacting to touch — rather than simple color-change hovers.

PART 2 — Application Interface (chat-style product layout)

A structured application UI, modeled after modern AI chat products (Claude, ChatGPT) — NOT a traditional scattered dashboard. Three fixed zones plus a scrollable center:

Left sidebar (fixed, persistent): A history/conversation-style list — vertical list of past entries, each showing a compact label, a timestamp, and a small status indicator. Should feel like a chat app's conversation history — clean rows, subtle hover states, clear active/selected state with a soft metallic highlight, not just a color change. Include a search/filter input at the top of the sidebar.

Top bar (fixed, persistent): Product wordmark/logo on the left, a row of selectable pill/tab buttons in the center or right (representing different data categories/modes) — selected state should have a polished chrome/glow treatment, unselected states should be subdued dark gray.

Main center content (scrollable): This is the primary content area — a large, prominent metric/result display (a big number or gauge as the visual focal point), supporting data displayed in a clean small-multiple stat grid below it (a row of small boxed stats), and an expandable/collapsible detailed content section below that (accordion-style panels with subtle chrome borders). Include a secondary content block further down for comparative/analytical data — presented as a clean bar or line chart with a minimal, monochrome style (no colored data series — use different opacities/patterns of white/gray to distinguish data instead of color).

Bottom bar (fixed, persistent): A chat-style input bar spanning the width of the main center content area only (not the sidebar) — rounded pill shape, placeholder text, a send button on the right. Should look and feel exactly like a modern AI chat product's message input — always visible regardless of scroll position in the center content.

Depth and materiality throughout the app: Avoid flat, uniform gray boxes. Every card/panel should have a sense of physical material — soft gradients suggesting a curved or brushed metal surface, subtle inner or outer glow on borders, soft shadows for real depth. The interface should feel like it's carved from or coated in liquid metal, not just "dark mode with gray boxes."

Overall goal: the two parts (landing page and app) should feel like one continuous, premium product — same material language, same monochrome palette, same confident typography — with the landing page as the cinematic introduction and the app as the polished, functional product experience.
